# Prof. Dusit Niyato: background and publications (read 2026-11-28)

Sources: his homepage https://personal.ntu.edu.sg/dniyato/ (self-reported; NTU directory page did not load) and OpenAlex author A5091266202 ("Dusit Tao Niyato", about 2,400 works, about 125,000 citations; OpenAlex lists a non-NTU last institution, which is an OpenAlex data issue).

## Title and honors (homepage)
- President's Chair Professor in Computer Science and Engineering (Nov 2022 - present); Professor, College of Computing and Data Science (CCDS), since Sep 2017.
- IEEE Fellow (2017, ComSoc) "for contributions to resource allocation in cognitive radio and cellular wireless networks". IET Fellow (2022). CIC Fellow (2025). Clarivate Highly Cited Researcher (Computer Science), 2017-2024 (not checked against Clarivate).
- Past Editor-in-Chief, IEEE Communications Surveys & Tutorials (2021-2024).
- Stated interests: "mobile generative AI, edge general intelligence, quantum computing and networking, and incentive mechanism design."
