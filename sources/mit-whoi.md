# MIT-WHOI Joint Program: Requirements and Faculty (read 2026-11-22)

## Requirements (official pages)
- Deadline: December 1, 2026, 11:59 PM EST ("anticipated"). = December 2, 12:59 PM Singapore time.
- Statement of objectives: recommended 1.5 to 2.5 pages, single-spaced. Why grad school, what to study, prior research. Can name faculty.
- CV: required.
- Transcripts: unofficial, non-encrypted PDFs. Describe grading system of each school.
- Letters: 3. Names and emails in the form. Late letters possible, review not guaranteed.
- GRE: not required.
- English: waiver if English was primary language in primary and secondary school. Waiver page: https://mit.whoi.edu/admissions/apply/waivers/ (not read yet).
- Research areas: list up to 3, in order. AOSE is one.
- One JP application. A separate MIT department application (e.g., EECS) needs its own form and fee.
- Fee: amount not stated. Fee waiver deadline Nov 16 (passed).
- Advisor: not required. Faculty contact encouraged. Email: interests, CV, unofficial transcript, availability. CC the Education Coordinator for the discipline.
- Reviewers consider "a match with potential advisors who have funding in hand".
- Funding: admitted students get stipend and tuition.
- No interviews. Offers mid-late February to mid-March.
- Admitted students can start in June or September. June strongly encouraged.
