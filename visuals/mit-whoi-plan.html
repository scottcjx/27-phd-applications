Rules from the official pages

  • MIT-WHOI: statement of objectives, 1.5 to 2.5 pages, single-spaced. CV. Unofficial PDF transcripts and a description of each grading scale. 3 letters. No GRE.
  • English: MIT-WHOI waives the test if English was your main language in primary and secondary school. Use the waiver form.
  • Research areas: list up to 3, in order. Put Applied Ocean Science & Engineering first.
  • Advisor: you do not have to name one. But the reviewers look for "a match with potential advisors who have funding in hand".
  • Start: admitted students can start in June. The program strongly encourages a June start.
  • Stanford EE: exactly 3 letters, no GRE. Stanford ME: 3 letters, at least 1 academic. The ME page does not accept the GRE.
  • US holiday: Thanksgiving is Nov 26 to 27. US faculty and Oliver Armitage (Cambridge, MA) may reply slowly on those days.
